Material for **Week 01** of the Coffee & Code track, a technology club founded by Software Engineering students at PUCPR and open to anyone who wants to learn by building.

This week exists to show something almost nobody tells you at the start: **a software project does not begin with writing code**. Before the first line, there is a problem to understand, a user to get to know, requirements to write and screens to design. That is what you will do here.

By the end of the week you will not have running code, and that is fine. You will have something that is usually missing: clarity about what to build and why.

> **About the language:** the English material lives in [`docs-eng/`](docs-eng/), with the same structure as [`docs/`](docs/) (Portuguese); the READMEs have an English version with the `.en.md` suffix. The example project and the file names you create in your own repository (such as `requisitos.md`) stay in Portuguese, so they match the rest of the track.

## The deliverable

At the end of Week 01, the repository of **your project** (not this one) should have:

**1. A `/docs` folder** with:

```text
/docs
├── requisitos.md
├── historias-de-usuario.md
├── design-system.md
└── arquitetura.md    ← initial version; explored further by experienced members
```

**2. A clickable prototype in Figma**, with the screens of the main flow connected.

It does not need to be perfect. It needs to exist and make sense with what you defined during the week. The details are in [docs-eng/deliverable.md](docs-eng/deliverable.md).

## Two tracks, one project

Throughout the modules you will find two kinds of sections:

- **If you are just starting**: explanations at a calm pace, without assuming you have seen it before.
- **If you already have experience**: deeper dives, good practices and extra challenges.

They are not two courses. Everyone works on the same project; only the depth changes. If you already code, the beginner sections are still worth reading: you may find gaps in the fundamentals that we tend to skip.

## How it works

Coffee & Code is **100% online**. Each module was written to be self-contained: you study at your own pace, and you can move ahead faster, go back to previous weeks and look things up during the project.

The weekly meetings, also online, are there to answer questions, review concepts, code together and show what you have built, **not to lecture** (Brasília time, UTC-3):

- **Wednesday**: 8:00 PM to 9:30 PM
- **Saturday**: 10:00 AM to 11:30 AM

Both cover the same content. Pick whichever fits your week best, and you do not need to stay on the call for the whole time.

## Stuck?

Bring a specific question to the meeting or to Discord. `"I got stuck on X, I already tried Y"` usually gets solved much faster than `"I didn't understand anything in module Z"`.

And remember: not knowing something is not a problem. Knowing how to research is part of the field.
